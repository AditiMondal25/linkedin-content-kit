AI BANNED WORDS AND PHRASES — never use these in posts
(This file is ready to use as-is. Add your own pet peeves at the bottom.)

Words:
delve, unlock, unleash, leverage, synergy, elevate, empower, harness, navigate (as metaphor),
landscape, realm, tapestry, journey (as metaphor), game-changer, revolutionize, cutting-edge,
seamless, robust, holistic, pivotal, paramount, transformative, groundbreaking, supercharge,
streamline (overused), dive in, deep dive, embark, foster, bustling, testament, intricate,
meticulous, ever-evolving, dynamic (as filler), crucial, vital

Phrases:
- "In today's fast-paced world"
- "In the ever-evolving landscape of"
- "Let's dive in" / "Let's unpack this"
- "Have you ever...?" (as an opening hook)
- "What do you think?" / "Thoughts?" (as a closing line)
- "Here's the thing"
- "It's not just X, it's Y"
- "At the end of the day"
- "I'm thrilled / humbled / excited to announce"
- "Agree?"
- "Game-changing"

AI tells (2026 list):
- Reveal bridges: "The result?" / "The catch?" / "Plot twist:"
  Write the reveal as a plain sentence with a number in it instead.
- "Here's what / Here's how / Here's why" as an opener
- "Stop X, start Y"
- Sincerity announcements: "Honestly?", "Let me be honest", "Real talk", "Unpopular opinion:"
  Just state the honest fact. No announcing it.
- "Most people don't realize..." / "The hard truth is..."
- "Let that sink in" / "That's the real story"
- "Tag someone who needs this" / "Let me know in the comments!"
- Filler words: quietly, comprehensive, insights, nuanced, multifaceted, notably,
  particularly, fundamentally, essentially, ultimately, utilize, facilitate, ecosystem

Style rules:
- Never open a post with a question. Put the question at the end.
- Max 1 contrast ("not X, it's Y") and 1 list-of-three per post.
- Max 2 fragment sentences under 4 words per post.
- Em dashes: at most 1 per post. Use full stops.
- No more than 3 hashtags.
- No emoji bullet lists of 5+ items.
- No rhetorical triple ("faster, smarter, better").
